3 · Per-joint actuator map

13 powered HingeJoint2D motors drive a 16-part 2D ragdoll (4-segment spine + 2 legs + 2 arms + 2 toes). Two unpowered toe MTP hinges are appended after the action indices and carry limits + passive spring only. Motor torque is scaled per step by Hill's force-velocity curve, an activation lag (τrise 0.05 s / τfall 0.07 s) and the fatigue integrator (1 − 0.35 · fatigue).

8 · Behavior descriptions

Matt — aggressive baseline

Behavior name Matt. Physique (1.00, 1.00, 1.00) → 69.6 kg at 1.76 m. The original PoSumo wrestler retuned into the aggressive lightweight: highest impact reward on the roster (0.015 cap 8). He wins by driving forward and hitting hard. Walk style is longer-strided and more committed than Standard's. The face art fallback constants in Systems_FaceMood resolve his head photos — his character asset leaves the face name fields empty.

Standard — neutral reference

Behavior name Standard. Physique (1.00, 1.00, 1.00). Default reward shaping — every other character is defined by how it deviates from this one. He is the highest-ELO fighter on the roster (5941 at 15 M, per his MANIFEST), which is worth reading carefully: it is a self-play number, not a claim that he beats the others in the game. He also trained the longest against an unbroken opponent pool. No face art and no voice clips — the audio/visual systems disable themselves silently rather than warn. The highest cumulative reward (4.15 at 2.76 M) is the canonical "default" reference for shaping comparisons.

Nick — mobile lightweight

Behavior name Nick. Physique (0.72, 0.82, 0.85) → ~50 kg. The smallest body on the roster and the highest cadence reward (0.0032): he dances, steps and picks his moment instead of grinding forward. His run crashed at 3.75 M and was resumed, which reset the self-play ELO scale to the initial_elo: 1200 baseline — his final ELO 4581 is therefore measured against a pool that began from scratch at 3.75 M, while the other three accumulated theirs across a full 15 M. Judge on ELO shape, not level. Reward-hack risk: a pre-merge re-tune climbed to mean reward ~36 while ELO fell 1198 → 1140. Cut shaping on the asset if you retrain him; do not just train him longer.

Kim — heavyweight anchor

Behavior name Kim. Physique (1.45, 1.30, 1.50) → ~101 kg with a sumo belly. The opposite pole from Nick: deep planted stance, does not chase, raw impact over cadence. straightLegEarnFraction 0.15 means she must crouch to be paid. Her run shipped at 15 M cold (no pre-merge checkpoints survive the 44 → 45 observation bump). No voice clips. Manifest claims gamma 0.99 but YAML ships gamma 0.997 — the YAML wins per CLAUDE.md and should be reflected back into the manifest on next edit.
