Coupling to be aware of. CollectObservations builds the joint block by looping over ActionCount. Adding a 14th motorised joint would change the action space and the observation vector together, invalidating every brain's input and output layer at once. This is why the proposed neck joint must be passive.

6 · Diagnostics

Action-space saturation — resolved
