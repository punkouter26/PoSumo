Why everything is retraining. The fighters' bodies were rebuilt to match real human proportions — legs were 8–18% too short, joints bent backwards further than a person can, and the shoulders and spine were 2–4× too strong. The ring also doubled in size. A brain trained on the old body can't drive the new one, so all eight are being re-taught. The old brains still work in-game until the new ones land; nothing was deleted.

The big win

The fighters used to slam every muscle to maximum, constantly — which is why they looked robotic. One reward was accidentally making effort free whenever they moved fast.
