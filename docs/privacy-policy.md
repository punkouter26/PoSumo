# Privacy Policy for PoSumo

**Last updated: 5 August 2026**

PoSumo ("the app") is published by punkoutersoftware. This policy explains what
the app does with your information. The short version: it does not collect any.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** PoSumo does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
information or device data.

Specifically, the app does not:

- ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details
- collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts
- collect or use your location
- collect device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID
- contain advertising or advertising SDKs
- contain analytics, crash-reporting, or telemetry SDKs
- transmit anything to us or to any third party

## Network access

PoSumo is fully playable offline and contains no online functionality. The app
sends no data to us or to any third party at any time.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores your local game settings — such as whether sound is enabled — on
your device only, using standard Android app storage. This never leaves your
device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Children's privacy

PoSumo collects no data from anyone, including children. The app contains no
advertising, no in-app purchases, and no communication features of any kind.

## Third-party services

PoSumo is built with the Unity engine and uses Unity's on-device machine-learning
inference to drive its computer-controlled opponents. This runs entirely on your
device. Unity Analytics and Unity Ads are **not** enabled in this app, and no
Unity data-collection service is included in the build.

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google collects its own data in
connection with app distribution, governed by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We receive no
personal information from Google beyond aggregate, anonymous install and crash
statistics shown in the Play Console.

## Permissions

PoSumo requests no Android runtime permissions.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it. Because
the app collects nothing, material changes are unlikely.
